# Proof architecture — from rectangles to EPPA and DLF

This is a map of the mathematical arguments **actually implemented in Lean**. It is intended as a guide to locating proof obligations and their dependencies, not as a replacement for the checked proofs.

## A. The group-valued rectangle and exact relational presentation

The foundational [affine anti-involution](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Algebra/AffineReversal.lean) is allowed to have $s(1)\ne1$. The horizontal displacement relation is the genuinely noncommutative equality
$$
F(x_0,y_0)^{-1}F(x_0,y_1)=F(x_1,y_0)^{-1}F(x_1,y_1).
$$
[`CornerAmalgamation.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Algebra/CornerAmalgamation.lean), [`Gauge.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Algebra/Gauge.lean), and [`Rectangle.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Algebra/Rectangle.lean) establish the rank-one algebra. [`Language.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/Language.lean) packages **all ordered raw relations**, not just their group-valued images. [`RelationalRoundtripEquality.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/RelationalRoundtripEquality.lean) proves raw-to-relational roundtrip equality.

## B. Unconditional finite SAP

1. [`LiteralOnePointSAP.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/LiteralOnePointSAP.lean) and [`RelativeOnePointSAP.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/RelativeOnePointSAP.lean) handle two distinct new vertices over a common finite base. The proof separates new-part, same-old-part and two-old-part corner cases; the required effective colour in the last case is forced by $F(x,y)=F(x,b)F(a,b)^{-1}F(a,y)$. Raw reversal is handled separately.
2. [`OnePointChainSAP.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/OnePointChainSAP.lean) and [`FiniteChainExactBaseMap.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/FiniteChainExactBaseMap.lean) prove that the successive amalgamation squares paste **with the prescribed common-base map**, not an arbitrary isomorphic copy.
3. [`FinitePrefixOnePointChain.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/FinitePrefixOnePointChain.lean), [`PrefixChainExactBaseMap.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/PrefixChainExactBaseMap.lean) and [`FinitePrefixBoundaryEquiv.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/FinitePrefixBoundaryEquiv.lean) decompose every canonical finite extension $X\sqcup\mathrm{Fin}(m)$ into an actual chain of induced original-language one-point extensions.
4. [`CanonicalOnePointAgainstFiniteSAP.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/CanonicalOnePointAgainstFiniteSAP.lean) and [`OnePointAgainstCanonicalFiniteSAP.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/OnePointAgainstCanonicalFiniteSAP.lean) perform the canonical one-point-versus-finite amalgamation.
5. [`FiniteExtensionNormalForm.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/FiniteExtensionNormalForm.lean), [`CanonicalToRelativeOnePointSAP.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/CanonicalToRelativeOnePointSAP.lean) and [`StrongSquareRenaming.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/StrongSquareRenaming.lean) normalize arbitrary given finite-carrier embeddings and transfer the square **exactly** back to those embeddings.
6. [`FiniteSAPExactSecondInduction.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/FiniteSAPExactSecondInduction.lean) iterates along the other finite extension. The terminal [`RawDatum.finiteRelationalStrongSAP`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/FiniteRelationalStrongSAPComplete.lean) discharges all intermediate assumptions.

At every step, [`StrongSquare`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/StrongSquare.lean) retains *commutativity, exact overlap and joint carrier coverage*. The independently checked [relational JEP](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/FiniteRelationalJEP.lean) comes from disjoint raw union. The terminal SAP and JEP proofs passed the [final integration build](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/actions/runs/40342181226) and [JEP build](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/actions/runs/40337127618).

## C. Ordinary EPPA: two layers, one finite witness

The proof retains the explicit valuation construction rather than invoking a general Herwig–Lascar/HKN theorem.

- **Effective layer:** [`Valuations/EffectiveCarrier.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Valuations/EffectiveCarrier.lean), [`EffectiveAutomorphisms.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Valuations/EffectiveAutomorphisms.lean) and [`EffectiveEPPA.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Valuations/EffectiveEPPA.lean) extend partial automorphisms of the group-valued skeleton while preserving the rank-one rectangle law.
- **Raw refinement:** the effective image $\phi(c)$ does not determine the original $c$ when $\phi$ is not injective. [`Algebra/FiniteAffineResidualCode.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Algebra/FiniteAffineResidualCode.lean), [`Structure/RawCover.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/RawCover.lean) and [`RawCoverPartialResidualLift.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/RawCoverPartialResidualLift.lean) handle these genuinely raw distinctions.
- **One witness / all partial maps:** [`Structure/RawEPPA.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/RawEPPA.lean) chooses a fixed finite raw witness before quantifying partial automorphisms. [`RelationalRawEPPA.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/RelationalRawEPPA.lean) transfers this to the literal original relations; [`Bridge/RawTypedEPPA.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Bridge/RawTypedEPPA.lean) supplies genuine upstream typed `IsEPPAWitness`.

## D. Coherent EPPA: fixed covers and partial-isomorphism groupoids

The extra coherence condition is not a consequence of separate extensions. The positive construction uses finite stabilizer group lifts, a single common witness, and a functorial groupoid assembly, retaining all raw colours. See [`Bridge/GlobalRawFunctorFromGroupAndConnectors.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Bridge/GlobalRawFunctorFromGroupAndConnectors.lean) and [`Bridge/RawExactFunctorToTypedCoherent.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Bridge/RawExactFunctorToTypedCoherent.lean).

For $G=1$, [`Bridge/GOneTypedCoherentEPPA.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Bridge/GOneTypedCoherentEPPA.lean) provides coherence without requiring reversal-fixed raw colours. For nontrivial $G$, [`Bridge/NontrivialSaturatedTypedCoherentEPPA.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Bridge/NontrivialSaturatedTypedCoherentEPPA.lean) handles $S\ne\varnothing$ with *global* congruence saturation. Necessity is supplied by finite 2+1 / 3+1 obstruction gadgets; see [`Bridge/CoherentFiniteRawClassNecessity.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Bridge/CoherentFiniteRawClassNecessity.lean). The precise source-facing classification is [`FiniteCanonicalUniverseCoherentClassification.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Bridge/FiniteCanonicalUniverseCoherentClassification.lean).

## E. Semi-coherence and DLF: a weaker orbit condition

A **single complete good orbit** suffices for the positive relative safe-group construction. This is a smaller requirement than coherent EPPA's global orbit saturation.

- [`DLFSafePairAgeJointSuccessorWithConnectors.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/DLFSafePairAgeJointSuccessorWithConnectors.lean) and the corresponding safe-pair tower build genuine finite subgroup actions and all required connectors.
- [`DLFSafePairTowerLimitHomogeneity.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/DLFSafePairTowerLimitHomogeneity.lean) and [`DLFSafePairTowerActualDLFCriterion.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/DLFSafePairTowerActualDLFCriterion.lean) give a countable vertex/group limit, actual automorphism action, full age, homogeneity, density and local finiteness.
- Necessity is obtained by explicit finite orbit/partial-map gadgets and the absence of appropriate involutions; see [`DLFNegativeCompleteNecessity.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/DLFNegativeCompleteNecessity.lean).
- The **unrestricted** converse is not assumed from Sági: [`DLFUnrestrictedSemiCoherentClassification.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/DLFUnrestrictedSemiCoherentClassification.lean) constructs an actual countable selected-family tower and its locally finite dense subgroup.
- [`NativeRelationalSemiCoherentConverse.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Bridge/NativeRelationalSemiCoherentConverse.lean) and [`DLFRelationalClassification.lean`](https://github.com/shulda/lean_eppa_semigeneric/blob/df21cfa98a668e1a95dceb73ce83d852336e6842/SemigenericEPPA/Structure/DLFRelationalClassification.lean) make this entirely literal in the original `E,R_c` language.

## What this construction does not import

The upstream `AllThoseEPPA` dependency supplies typed structures, embeddings and partial-isomorphism/coherence definitions. It is **not** used as an external EPPA existence axiom. The project does not prove a general Sági theorem for arbitrary ages or formalize the optional converse classification of arbitrary abstract rectangle laws.
